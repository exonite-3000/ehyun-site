Alien: Earth

Logo Animation Exploration · FX
Animation, Design, Style Frames

Overview

For Alien: Earth, I developed a logo animation exploration focused on translating the show's unsettling, otherworldly tone into motion. The goal was to explore how rhythm, texture, and transformation could communicate a sense of alien intelligence and environmental threat, while remaining flexible enough to support multiple use cases across the brand.

This case study highlights my process, from early visual research and motion tests to iterative refinement and narrative-driven animation decisions. While the final animation was not selected, the project demonstrates my approach to concept development, experimentation, and problem-solving within an established creative direction.
